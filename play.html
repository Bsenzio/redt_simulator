1. Load the example

Download the CSV. In Data, select Open training file and keep Use timestamps enabled.

2. Train on the CSV

With the CSV loaded, open Training and press Train MPPT. The model learns a voltage target from the file.

3. Choose a panel

After training, select a panel in Field and set MPPT to isolated panel to test it on its own.
