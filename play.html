REDT Simulator

Simulator quick guide

Open Data to load a local CSV or JSON file, or generate a sample day. In Plot, Full series shows the whole file without running the scene.

In Training, add your series and choose how many to use. Generate variants if you want synthetic weather profiles. Select Train MPPT.

In Field, choose a panel or the series string. Select Play to run the same conditions for all controllers.

In Compare, choose Energy period. Compare total Wh for P&O, hill climbing, PID, fixed voltage, and the trained model. Use Power charts to see the traces.

The simulator files are being prepared.

For keyboard controls, click inside the simulator first. The browser version uses sample data or local files. Live UDP requires the desktop version.
